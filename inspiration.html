Example answers

Questions

The things people usually want to know before ordering.

Is there a minimum order?

Five boxes. For fewer, just ask and we’ll see what we can do.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Yes. Tell us about gluten-free, dairy-free, vegan, nut allergies or no-alcohol preferences when you ask for a quote, and we’ll swap items so those people get a box that’s just as good. Our boxes don’t include alcohol.

Can we add our logo or our own message?

Yes. Every box comes with a printed card with your message, and we send it to you to approve first. A logo seal on every box is $3 per box.

How do we pay?

You get one invoice for the whole order, paid by bank transfer. We ask for 50% when you confirm and the rest on delivery.

Do your prices include GST?

Prices are per box. We are not GST registered, so there is no GST to add.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver to your workplace in and around Whangārei. We can also courier boxes to each person anywhere in New Zealand, from $12 per address.

What if we miss the 4 December deadline?

Get in touch anyway. If we have room, we’ll fit you in.
